# Facilitator training playbook

Synthetic, non-accredited training. Suggested duration: 75–90 minutes. No real personal or confidential data.

1. **Frame the decision (10 min):** identify decision owner, affected people, alternatives, constraints, and evidence needed. Create a problem brief and stakeholder record.
2. **Inspect data (15 min):** open source versions and dictionary. Import the defect fixture in a disposable demo project; inspect all eight rejections and download the report. Discuss why structural validity is not accuracy.
3. **Medicine workflow (15 min):** compare usable stock, coverage, expiry, and forecast limitations. Ask why zero consumption must not imply infinite coverage. Inspect a redistribution candidate and its reserve/transit constraints.
4. **Finance workflow (10 min):** switch currency; trace an unmatched payment. Discuss why an anomaly is not proof of fraud and why currencies are not summed.
5. **Allocation trade-offs (10 min):** compare budgets and equity/geography constraints. Inspect an infeasible scenario. Discuss overlapping reach and assumed benefits.
6. **Human review (15 min):** manager submits a proposal; independent reviewer approves/rejects/defers with reason; operator records implementation/outcome separately. Emphasise that no stock or money moves.
7. **Responsible AI (10 min):** ask the local assistant a grounded question and an unsupported question. Inspect citations and limitations. Record a mock incident and remediation.

Assessment: learners must identify evidence, assumptions, limitations, and the accountable human reviewer. Academy scores record internal progress only, not formal accreditation or competence certification.
