# Responsible AI and decision governance

## Intended use

Support investigation, explanation, and human review of development-programme evidence. The demo uses fictional countries, facilities, financial records, and intervention assumptions. It does not represent any real government, patient, health system, or partner.

## Prohibited use

Autonomous medicine or fund transfers; fraud accusations from anomalies; invented citations or operational facts; clinical diagnosis; patient-level inference; policy rankings presented as final decisions; synthetic benefits presented as causal evidence; credentials implying accreditation; uploading confidential or unlawfully obtained data to the demo.

## Accountability

Recommendations retain owner, evidence, assumptions, limitations, and state. Review decisions snapshot original/final action and evidence, require a reason, and prohibit self-approval. Implementation is a separate operator record and does not execute an external transaction. Outcome notes are not verified impact measurement. Audit records are append-only for the application role.

## Fairness and context

Synthetic geography/equity constraints demonstrate trade-offs, not a validated fairness policy. Real deployment requires local stakeholder participation, disaggregated error analysis, accessibility and language review, lawful data assessment, and a contestability/appeal process. An anomaly is a prompt to verify records, not evidence of misconduct. Correlation is not causation.

## Privacy and external AI

No patient data is seeded. Learning progress and notifications are private to their user. Sensitive evidence is excluded from external calls; source-version presence is required. External explanation is disabled by default. A provider key alone does not enable calls. Organisations must review provider retention, region, subprocessors, and legal basis before sending even aggregate private data.

## Human oversight and incident response

Governance includes risk assessments, incidents, model information, and audit history. For a suspected harmful output: stop using the recommendation, preserve evidence/audit references, record an incident, notify the owner, revoke access if needed, correct the source or method, and require independent review before reuse. Do not silently edit immutable evidence.

## Learning

Academy completion records an internal assessment score only. It is not formal accreditation, professional certification, or proof of competence.
